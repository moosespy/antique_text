# Search for Sherlock
You've woken up in the 1800s, and something's wrong - you don't know who you are, where you are, or how you got there. A man finds you, and your small adventure starts to find out who you are! The goal is to find out who you are.

Controls: when prompted, type 'n', 'e', 's', or 'w' to move to a different section of the city.

There is only one ending to the game for now, more may come later.
