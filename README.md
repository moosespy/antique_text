# Search for Sherlock
You've woken up in the 1800s, and something's wrong - you don't know who you are, where you are, or how you got there. A man finds you, and your small adventure starts to find out who you are! The goal is to find out who you are.

Controls: when prompted, type 'n', 'e', 's', or 'w' to move to a different section of the city.
To run the game, open the demo link and press 'Run'.

The game works in a coordinate system, where behind the scenes, every time you press 'n', your y-coordinate increases by 1, and similarly, 's' decreases the y-coordinate by 1, and 'e' and 'w' increase and decrease the 'x' coordinate by 1 respectively.

There are now 4 endings to the game, more may come later. Although the goal is to find out who you are, also try to find all the details of your past by finishing multiple endings...

This was made in Python, as one of my first projects. This introduced me to a coordinate system that I could use to move the player around in, which is much cleaner than my original idea, which was to have each action take you to a different part of the storyline, not necessarily geography-based. It was more like a Pick-The-Plot book, and that didn't look as clean at all.

I made this because I wanted to try a true text adventure game where you are working towards a goal. My first project for Terra was also a terminal game, but more of a minigame-based one, and I didn't have an end goal of any sort.
